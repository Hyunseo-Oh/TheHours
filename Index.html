      Take it to another device

      This makes one file that holds the app and a copy of your records together. Open it on the iPad and everything is there — no importing.
      1. Press the button. Save the file to iCloud Drive.
      2. On the iPad, open Files and tap that file. It opens in Safari with your records already in it.
      3. Record as usual while you are out.
      4. Back home, export JSON from the iPad and bring it into this copy with Merge.
      Make a fresh file each time you go out, so you carry the latest records. Dragging cards between days needs a mouse; on the iPad, tap a card and set its date instead.
